## 6. Statistical precision

* Main study: 500 paired seeds per cell; the widest 95% interval on a pass rate is ±4.3 pp.
* Approximate planning target for ±5 pp: 385 runs per cell; ±2 pp: 2401 runs per cell (worst case p = 50%).
* The convergence plot is descriptive. Its pointwise intervals are not a sequential stopping rule; use a predeclared sample count and independent replication.

## 7. Beacon power assumptions (engineering estimate)

Assuming 12 mW standby plus a 1.1 W incremental transmit load for 15 ms at 1 Hz gives 28.5 mW average and 20.52 Wh over 30 days. These are design assumptions, not hardware measurements. A 25 Wh usable-energy allocation has about 22% margin before aging, temperature, conversion losses and radiation derating. Battery mass, RF link margin and qualification remain unverified.

## Limitations (always quote with results)

* Linearised Clohessy–Wiltshire relative motion; no J2, solar pressure or third-body effects.
* Planar (yaw-only) attitude estimator; the true client may nutate a few degrees off-axis.
* Terminal window ≠ physical capture: no contact, latch, flexible-appendage or plume models.
* All sensor, beacon and actuator numbers are illustrative assumptions, not ClearSpace data.
* Pass rates are conditional on the studied scenario grid, not fleet-wide probabilities.
